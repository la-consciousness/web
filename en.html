About

Building durable scientific infrastructure for the region.

The Latin American Society for the Scientific Study of Consciousness is a regional initiative in formation. It aims to connect researchers, clinicians, trainees and institutions, reduce fragmentation across countries and disciplines, and strengthen Latin America’s contribution to consciousness science.

Working mission

Connect, strengthen and increase visibility

Strengthen research, education and public communication; promote collaboration across countries and disciplines; and make Latin America’s cultural, linguistic and epistemic diversity a substantive contribution to the field.

An interdisciplinary bridge

The initiative brings together neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, medicine and clinical practice, computational and mathematical approaches, biology, philosophy and related fields.

Current status: the society’s formal structure, governance and membership remain under collective development.
